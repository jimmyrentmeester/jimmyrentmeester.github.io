BATTLEFOLD

// sea battle for two, on one foldable

Sit across the table from each other with one iPhone Duo between you. Stand it up like a laptop: your tracking grid upright, your own fleet flat on the table. Aim, hold to fire, and feel the torpedo land. Then fold it shut, turn it to your opponent, and they open it for the counterattack.

Coming to the App Store

On the iPhone Duo's inner screen, in the laptop stand.

What's in it

  • Folding the device shut is the turn change: closed, it only shows a neutral periscope view
  • Demo (6×6, a hit earns another shot) or Classic (10×10, Salvo)
  • Place your fleet yourself, or shuffle
  • Every shot is a moment: torpedo trail, impact, sinking hulls, sound and haptics
  • A guided practice round to learn it in a minute
  • Rematch in one tap, with a running score of who's winning
  • 15 Game Center achievements
  • Also plays on other iPhones, with a button instead of the fold
  • No account, no ads, no tracking
