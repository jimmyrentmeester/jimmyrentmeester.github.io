BATTLEFOLD

// privacy policy

BattleFold has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. I do not collect, receive or store any data about you. The only thing that can leave your device is your achievement progress, if you are signed in to Game Center. That is explained below.

What the app stores

The game saves the player names, the running score between you, your settings and your achievement progress locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). Nothing is stored about where you placed your ships after a match. This data is removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

BattleFold uses Apple's Game Center for achievements. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, your achievement progress is sent to Apple. That is the whole exchange.

This is handled entirely by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy. I have no server involved and I receive nothing. If you are not signed in to Game Center, or you turn it off in iOS Settings, the game plays exactly the same and nothing is sent anywhere.

The fold sensor

On the iPhone Duo, the game reads whether the device is open or closed, and the hinge angle, to know when the turn changes. This is used only on the device, in the moment, and is never stored or sent.

What I don't do

  • No personal information is collected or requested.
  • No servers of my own. There is no BattleFold account and no backend.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • No access to camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.

Children

The app collects nothing about anyone, children included. Note that Game Center is Apple's service: parents can restrict or disable it under iOS Screen Time.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
